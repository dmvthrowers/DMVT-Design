More Than a Yo-Yo Club

DMV Throwers is the Washington DC/Maryland/Virginia region's community hub for yo-yo and skill toy players. Est. 2021.

Who We Are

We meet every 3rd Sunday at Arlington Central Library. All ages, all skill levels, always free. Whether you've never touched a yo-yo or you're grinding competition routines — this is your crew.

Identity

Voice & Tone

Our voice is consistent across all channels — direct, warm, never corporate. Adjust tone, not voice.

Direct
We say what we mean. Short sentences, no fluff. "All ages, all levels, always free."
Welcoming
Community-first. Second person always. "This is your crew."
Regional Pride
DC/MD/VA identity front and center. Celebrate the DMV.
Authentic
Real people, real candids. No stock. No performative language.
✓ Write like this
"Whether you've never touched a yo-yo or you're grinding competition routines."
"3rd Sunday every month. Arlington Central Library. Free — always."
"Find your local crew."
✕ Avoid this
"We are a premier skill toy organization committed to excellence."
"Join our community today and unlock your potential!"
"DMV Throwers Club welcomes all participants."

Identity

Logo Variations

Primary Red
Use on cream/white backgrounds. Default for print and web.
Reversed White
Use on navy, dark, or high-contrast backgrounds.

Specialty use: red CTAs, stickers, merchandise accents.
Black & White
For single-color printing, embroidery, and reproduction.
Glyph Only — Small
For favicons, social avatars, small lockups. Min 24px.
Deepest Dark
For VSYC-26 or very dark contexts.
Clear Space Rule
Always maintain minimum clear space equal to the logo glyph height × 0.5 on all sides. Never crowd the logo with text or competing graphics.

Identity

Logo Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do
Use on approved backgrounds only
Cream, white, navy, or brand red.
Maintain clear space
× 0.5 glyph height on all sides, minimum.
Scale proportionally
Never distort or stretch the logo.
Use white version on dark
Apply brightness(0) invert(1) filter.
Minimum size: 24px
For the glyph; 80px for full lockup.
✕ Don't
Alter colors
Don't recolor the glyph to any non-brand color.
Add effects
No drop shadows, glows, gradients, or outlines.
Skew or rotate
The logo must always appear upright and proportional.
Place on busy photography
Insufficient contrast destroys legibility.
Use old versions
Always source from the approved assets folder.

System

Design Elements

Dot Pattern
Used on dark navy surfaces. Radial gradient white dots, 28px grid, 6% opacity. Hero backgrounds, footers, dark callout cards.
radial-gradient(rgba(255,255,255,0.06) 1px,
transparent 1px) / 28px 28px
DC Skyline Ornament
SVG silhouette. Bottom-anchored in hero sections. Opacity 14–18%, white. Reinforces regional identity. Never above 20% opacity.
Red Rule / Divider
48px × 3px — after headings
Hairline — between sections
5px footer bar — page bottoms
Sharp Corners — Always
JOIN
border-radius: 0 always. Cards, buttons, tags, inputs — all sharp. Core brand motif. Never round corners.
