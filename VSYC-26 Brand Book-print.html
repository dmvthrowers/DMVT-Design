Identity

Logo Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do
Use VSYC-26 logo for contest materials
Never use the main DMV Throwers glyph for contest-specific contexts.
Use DMV Throwers logo for presenter credit
"Presented by DMV Throwers" always in footer/credits.
Maintain gold or white color only
On dark backgrounds. On light: full color logo only.
Apply sponsor logos per tier rules
Refer to sponsor tier sheet for sizing and placement.
✕ Don't
Mix logos on the same layout
VSYC and DMV logos must be clearly separated or in footer only.
Recolor with non-brand colors
No custom colors; gold, white, or navy only.
Add drop shadows or effects
No glows, bevels, gradients, or outlines.
Place on cherry-pink or busy imagery
Cherry pink is a background only; never behind the logo.

Identity

Voice & Tone

VSYC-26 shares DMV Throwers' direct, welcoming voice — but the tone shifts to official and elevated for competition contexts.

Official
Competition language is precise. Rulebook = rules. Division = division. Results are announced, not posted. Use formal register.
Inclusive
Still welcoming. Competitors of all levels are celebrated. Never intimidating. "Everyone is welcome on the floor."
Proud
Virginia state pride is prominent. "The Commonwealth's finest throwers." Regional identity matters here too.
Concise
Rules are clear, schedules are direct. No filler copy. Registration copy is action-oriented: "Register. Compete. Win."
✓ Write like this
"Registration is open. Compete in 5 divisions. Arlington VA, 2026."
"The Commonwealth's finest throwers. See you on the floor."
✕ Avoid this
"Come hang and throw with us at our super fun contest!"
"VSYC-26 is a premier world-class competition experience."

Division System

Five competition divisions, each with a gold badge, full name, and brief description. Always use Barlow Condensed 800 for division labels.

1A
String
String Trick
Single yo-yo on a string. Freestyle. The most common and classic division.
Open
2A
Loop
Looping
Two yo-yos, continuous looping tricks. High-speed, rhythmic performance.
Open
3A
Double
Double String
Two yo-yos, each on their own string. Complex, simultaneous manipulation.
Open
4A
Off
Offstring
Yo-yo not attached to the string. Aerial catches, wraps, and releases.
Open
5A
CW
Counterweight
Yo-yo on a string with a counterweight instead of a finger loop. Freehand style.
